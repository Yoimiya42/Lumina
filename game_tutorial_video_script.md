# Game Tutorial Video Script

## Opening

Hi welcome to this quick tutorial for Lumina.  
This is a game where your breathing gradually fills grey pictures with color.

---

## Main Script
Now we are in the menu. 
You can click **Show Color** to preview the fully colored pictures, and click **Show Gray** to switch it back to grayscale.

These buttons are albums; click them to expand the pictures below, then click one and choose a difficulty. The higher the difficulty, the smaller each painting cell, and the more cells you need to fill to complete the painting.
 
And we start.

### 1
The yellow circle shows the area you’re currently coloring.

You can click **Brush Size** to change the size of the painting area.

You can control it with your mouse, or by moving and holding your right hand, which gives it more of a real painting feel.

Please breathe in and out slowly and rhythmically, the color will gradually spread. 

When an area is completely filled in, its grid will disappear.

You can click Back or Quit at any time to leave the game, and your coloring progress will be saved automatically. 

If you want to start over, just click **Reset** to clear your previous difficulty setting and progress.

And once the progress bar on the left reaches **100%**, that means you’ve finished the picture.

### 2
After you launch the game for the first time, you’ll see a folder called `UserContent`. You can also create your own albums in the `UserContent\Lumina\Images` folder, add images you like, or remove photos you do not want.(For example, you can create a folder named `Summer` and put your images in it. They will automatically appear in the album list in the menu when you start the game.)
