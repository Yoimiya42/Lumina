# Lumina
*Breath-Driven Oil Painting-Style Coloring Game*

---

### Objective
- Use your breath to gradually restore color to a gray painting until the entire artwork is complete.
- Complete as many paintings as possible to win trophies!

---

### How to Play
**Before You Start:**
- Use the **mouse** or **your fingertips** to select a painting from an album
- Choose a **difficulty level**(The higher the difficulty, the smaller each painting cell, and the more cells you need to fill to complete the painting. ), optionally reset previous difficulty selection and progress, and **start** coloring.

**During Gameplay**:
1. Move the **mouse** or **your palm** to select an area to color.
  - The yellow circle shows your current painting area.You can move the painting area with your mouse, or control it by moving and holding your right hand, which makes it feel more like real painting.
  - You can click **Brush Size** to adjust the size of the painting area.
  - When an area is fully filled with color, the white grid lines will disappear.
2. Use steady, regular breathing to fill that area with color.
3. Repeat this process until the progress bar reaches 100%, and the painting is complete.

---

### Tips
- If you feel tired, you can click the `Back` button at any time. Your coloring progress will be saved, and you can continue from where you left off next time.

- After you launch the game for the first time, you’ll see a folder called `UserContent`. You can also create your own albums in the `UserContent\Lumina\Images` folder, add images you like, or remove photos you do not want.(For example, you can create a folder named `Summer` and put your images in it. They will automatically appear in the album list in the menu when you start the game.)

- If you do not want to use palm movement to control the coloring area or click buttons, you can uncheck `Enable Motion Input` in `Settings` before entering the game and use the mouse instead.
