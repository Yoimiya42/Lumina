# Lumina
*Breath-Driven Oil Painting-Style Coloring Game*

---
### Objective
- Use your breath to gradually restore color to a gray painting until the entire artwork is complete.
- Complete as many paintings as possible to win trophies!
---

### How to Play
**Before You Start:**
- Use the **mouse** or **your fingertips** to select a painting from an album
- Choose a difficulty level, optionally reset progress, and start coloring.

**During Gameplay**:
- Move the **mouse** or **your palm** to select an area to color.
- Use steady, regular breathing to fill that area with color.
- Repeat this process until the progress bar reaches 100%, and the painting is complete.
---

### Tips
- If you feel tired, you can click the `Back` button at any time. Your coloring progress will be saved, and you can continue from where you left off next time.

- You can also create your own albums in the `UserContent\Lumina\Images` folder, add images you like, or remove photos you do not want.
